Summary:
### Abstract Summary
The research paper introduces the concept of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GAI), which combines post-structuralistic theories of possibility spaces with neo-cybernetic mechanisms to create open-ended systems. The approach aims to bridge the gaps in Artificial Intelligence by integrating top-down and bottom-up mechanisms, allowing for the emergence of intelligent machines. The paper proposes a new paradigm for AI research, focusing on the manipulation of possibility spaces to scaffold the minds of intelligent machines.

### Key Findings
* The current state of Artificial Intelligence lacks a unifying theory, with multiple paradigms (top-down, bottom-up, and cybernetics) that have not led to the creation of intelligent machines.
* The integration of post-structuralism and neo-cybernetics can provide a new approach to AI research, focusing on the manipulation of possibility spaces and the emergence of complex systems.
* The concept of virtual-actual distinction, inspired by Deleuze's philosophy, allows for the description of phases spaces and the patterns and thresholds that characterize the behavior of complex systems.
* The use of computer simulations can enable the exploration of possibility spaces and the investigation of emergent properties, capacities, and tendencies in complex systems.
* The paper proposes a flat ontology, where different systems can be described in terms of virtual potentialities and actualizations, and where explanations can be built upon lower hierarchies through a process called bootstrapping.

### Methodology
The research paper employs a multidisciplinary approach, combining concepts from post-structuralism, neo-cybernetics, and Deleuze's philosophy to develop a new paradigm for AI research. The paper uses a theoretical framework to describe the concept of Generative Artificial Intelligence and its potential applications. The authors also draw on existing research in AI, cybernetics, and philosophy to support their arguments and propose new directions for AI research.

### Significance
The research paper contributes to the development of a new paradigm for Artificial Intelligence, one that focuses on the creation of open-ended systems and the emergence of intelligent machines. The proposed approach has the potential to bridge the gaps in current AI research and provide a new framework for understanding the complex interactions between systems and their environments.
